Footprinting & Reconnaissance

Passive and active attack-surface mapping, search engine discovery, DNS and WHOIS enumeration, social networking and social engineering reconnaissance, tooling, AI-assisted collection, and defensive countermeasures — building the target profile before any exploitation begins.

Module Overview

Footprinting and reconnaissance form the disciplined foundation of every authorized assessment and red-team engagement. Before a single exploit is attempted, operators map the organization’s external and internal attack surface using publicly available data, carefully scoped interactions, and structured intelligence methods.

This module covers the full reconnaissance workflow: search-engine and internet research techniques, social networking discovery, WHOIS and DNS enumeration, network and email footprinting, social engineering collection, advanced tooling and AI support, and the defensive countermeasures that raise the cost of real-world reconnaissance.

What You Will Cover

  • Foundations: Introduction to footprinting, core concepts, and passive vs active collection.
  • Public Discovery: Search engines, internet research services, and social networking reconnaissance.
  • Infrastructure Mapping: WHOIS, DNS, network, and email footprinting techniques.
  • Human Layer: Social engineering collection methods and operational caution.
  • Tooling & AI: Advanced reconnaissance tools and AI-assisted discovery workflows.
  • Defense: Countermeasures that reduce exposure and raise adversary cost.
  • Cheat Sheet: Consolidated reference for rapid operational recall.

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish passive, semi-active, and active footprinting within a structured reconnaissance cycle.
  • Map organizational attack surfaces using search engines, research services, and social platforms.
  • Enumerate domains, DNS records, WHOIS data, network ranges, and email infrastructure.
  • Apply social engineering reconnaissance techniques within authorized engagement boundaries.
  • Leverage advanced tools and AI support to accelerate discovery while preserving OPSEC.
  • Recommend and implement footprinting countermeasures that reduce public exposure.
