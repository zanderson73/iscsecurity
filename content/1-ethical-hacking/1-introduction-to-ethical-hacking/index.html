Introduction to Ethical Hacking

Learn the foundations of ethical hacking, information security, adversarial thinking, legal considerations, and security controls.

Module Overview

This module introduces the core principles of ethical hacking and offensive security. Students will learn the role of ethical hackers, common attack methodologies, information security concepts, legal responsibilities, and defensive security controls.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the role of ethical hacking.
  • Understand core information security concepts.
  • Recognize common attack methodologies.
  • Understand security controls and governance.
  • Understand legal and ethical responsibilities.
  • Prepare for advanced offensive security topics.
