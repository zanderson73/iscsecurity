Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ISCSECURITY collects, uses, and protects personal information when you access our website or Services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: account details, contact information, payment details (processed by payment providers), messages, and submissions.
  • Automatic data: device, browser, IP address, general location, and usage metrics (e.g., pages viewed, time on page).
  • Cookies & similar tech: used for session management, preferences, analytics, and security.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, secure, and improve the Services.
  • Process subscriptions and customer support requests.
  • Communicate updates and relevant content (you can opt out).
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce agreements.

3. Legal Bases (where applicable)

We process data based on consent, contract performance, legitimate interests (e.g., security, service improvement), and legal obligations.

4. Sharing & Transfers

We may share information with service providers under confidentiality and data protection commitments, including:

  • Stripe — payment processing. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • MemberSpace — membership management and authentication. See MemberSpace's Privacy Policy.
  • Netlify — website hosting and content delivery. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety. International transfers follow appropriate safeguards.

5. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function, including MemberSpace session cookies that keep you logged in and enable membership access.
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences and improve your experience.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may prevent access to member-only content.

6. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect data. However, no method is 100% secure.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

8. Your Rights

Subject to your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal data, and object to certain processing. To exercise rights, contact privacy@iscsecurity.org.

9. Children

The Services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the age defined by local law). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Questions? privacy@iscsecurity.org or legal@iscsecurity.org.
